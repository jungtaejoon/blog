## New

- **Cheapest skill route.** The "Skill route" button in the profession window plans the cheapest way from your skill to your target: orange always gives a point, yellow and green by chance. Learned and unlearned recipes are compared, including ones learned above your current skill; unlearned ones are marked.
- **Sale mode.** Count crafted results as no sale, a merchant sale, an auction sale (after the fee) or the higher of the two.
- **Route prices in one go.** Up to 50 items per auction request, so a few hundred items take seconds. Prices seen in the last 30 minutes are reused.
- **How to learn a recipe.** Unlearned recipe tooltips show the recipe item, the trainer and required skill, automatic learning, and older-version vendor and drop data.
- **Skill colours.** Tooltips show the yellow, green and grey ranks and the current skill-up chance; the skill-up filter picks orange, yellow and green separately.
- **Target skill.** When the profession reaches it, the game's repeat crafting stops and you are told in chat, with a raid warning and a sound.
- **Merchant shortfall.** Beside the merchant window, the selected recipe's materials that merchant sells, short for your craft count; bought only when you click.
- Material totals (unit × quantity) in the auction recipe tree, sorting by equip level, cheapest and dearest merchants, a new-version notice, and item tooltips anywhere on a card.

## Fixed

- About 1,200 recipes showed the Classic learning rank instead of the Forever one (e.g. Cured Medium Hide 100 instead of 75).
- Materials whose Forever item links carry extra fields were not deducted from what you own.
- Auction searches could hang for up to 90 seconds after closing the auction house; the deposit for several of one item now matches what is charged.
