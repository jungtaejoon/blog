## New

- **Item quality filter in the profession window.** Under "Item quality · any of" in the filter menu, pick poor, common, uncommon, rare or epic. Several picks show results of any of them, together with the other conditions such as skill-up and learned state.
- **Quality filter for crafted goods at the auction house.** Next to the list scope on the crafted goods page, pick all, common, uncommon, rare, epic, poor or uncommon and better. The choice is kept.
- **Each profession remembers your last recipe.** The recipe you picked last is kept per profession and per character. Switching profession tabs, reopening the window or filtering it out of the list selects it again.
- **Merchant shortfall panel on open.** Opening a merchant shows the shortfall for that recipe at once.

## Fixed

- The skill route did not appear after login until prices were looked up at the auction house. It now uses the most recent real prices saved, and says "Includes saved past prices · oldest N days ago" when older prices are mixed in.
- A material later found with no listing lost its earlier price and dropped out of the route.

## Windows app

- The Windows app 0.12.2 bundles addon 0.9.4. The app itself is the same as 0.12.0.
