## New

- **Craft plan.** The crafting workshop has a new "Craft plan" tab.
  - Add several recipes with craft counts and the plan totals their materials as needed, owned (bags) and short.
  - A material that another planned recipe makes is reduced by what the plan crafts: plan bolts of cloth together with a shirt that uses them and you buy fewer bolts.
  - Add recipes with "+ Add to craft plan" on the workshop, the button on the profession window's panel (left click +1, right click −1) or "+ Add selected recipe" on the plan page.
- **Buy the plan's shortfall at a merchant.** The shortfall window beside the merchant has "Craft plan" and "Selected recipe" tabs.
  - On the plan tab one click buys everything that merchant sells for the plan, in whole merchant bundles.
  - Short materials the merchant does not sell are listed separately and can be marked on the map.
  - It still buys only on your click and never in combat.
- **Buy plan materials at the auction house.** "Buy at auction" on the plan page opens the purchase page with the missing quantity filled in; you confirm the purchase. The shortfall can also go to a shopping list.
- **Merchants on the map.**
  - **Ctrl+click** a material anywhere (bags, the profession window's reagents, merchants, chat links) to mark the merchants selling it on the world map.
  - The click is a setting on the plan page: Ctrl+click, Ctrl+Alt+click, Alt+click or off.
  - Only merchants of your faction are shown. The nearest one per material gets a large star and the game's map pin.
  - A bar at the top of the map names the marked materials and the merchant count. Right click a material's icon to clear it, or "Clear all" for everything, including the map pin ForgeFlow placed.
  - Material tooltips show how many merchants sell it, the nearest one and the distance.
  - Merchant locations cover 39 materials and 520 merchants from WoW Forever data, plus merchants you or shared data have seen.
- **Auction sort by required level.** Item search results can be sorted by minimum required level, cheaper first on ties. The old "Level" sort is now "Item level". Rows show both levels.

## Changed

- **Gear: equip effects of items outside the data.** Equipped items missing from the gear list (quest rewards and the like) read their equip effects from the tooltip: school spell damage, spell damage and healing, healing, mana per 5 sec and the real weapon damage and speed.
- **Gear: caster wands.** Damage casters count wand damage for the time they are out of mana, at least a tenth of the fight.

## Windows app

- **Windows app 0.12.4** now installs addon 0.9.8.
