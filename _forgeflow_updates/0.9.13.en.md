## New

- **Take a recipe out of the skill route.**
  - Click × on a route row, or right-click it, to take that recipe out.
  - Only the stretch it covered is refilled with the next cheapest recipe; the rest stays.
  - Taken-out recipes are kept per character, and "Restore" at the top puts them back.
- **Recipes no trainer teaches are left out.** A recipe with no recipe item, no automatic learning and on no trainer list seen shows "How to learn unknown (on no trainer list)" and, if not learned, is left out of the skill route. Example: Blackfathom Sharpening Stone.

## Changed

- **Route price lookups become recent prices.** The lowest prices found by "Look up material and result prices" are the items' recent prices too, so the market, supply crate and workshop pages use them right away.
- **Look prices up again right away.** A price is reused for one minute instead of 30, and items with no listing are always looked up again.
- **Auction price in tooltips.** Without a current quote, tooltips show the last real price seen, such as "Lowest auction price 57s · 10 min ago", instead of "Auction price unknown".

## Windows app

- **Windows app 0.12.5** now installs addon 0.9.13. If you use 0.12.5, the app offers the new build.
