## New

- **Skill route: recipe colours.** Choose with the "Recipe colour" button at the bottom of the skill route panel.
  - Up to green (default): green recipes are used when they are cheaper even with their lower chance.
  - Up to yellow only: where a recipe turns green, the route moves on to another one.
  - Orange only: the route uses only orange recipes, which give a point every craft.
  - The alternative cards per stretch follow the same limit.
- **How to learn recipes in the craft-uses list.** The list opened with Alt on a material marks each recipe "Learned" or "Not learned".
  - Recipes you have not learned get a short note: learned automatically · skill 1, trainer · skill 15, recipe item · drop · skill 45.
  - Hover an unlearned recipe for the full details: the recipe item, its Forever sources and the trainer's place and cost.

## Changed

- **Craft plan per character.** Each character keeps its own plan. The account-wide plan from 0.9.8 moves to the first character that opens it.

## Windows app

- **Windows app 0.12.4** now installs addon 0.9.9.
