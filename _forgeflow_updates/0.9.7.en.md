## Changed

- **School spell power.** Gear recommendations count, for each main spell, only the spell power of that spell's school.
  - Mage: Frostbolt is frost, Fireball is fire
  - Warlock: Shadow Bolt is shadow
  - Shaman: Chain Lightning and Earth Shock are nature
  - Druid: Starfire is arcane, Wrath is nature
  - Priest: Mind Flay and Mind Blast are shadow, Smite is holy
  - Paladin: seals and judgements are holy
  - A frost mage no longer gains anything from "+60 fire spell damage".
- **School talents.** Talents for one school only apply to spells of that school: Fire Power to fire spells, Piercing Ice to frost spells, Darkness and Shadowform to shadow spells.
- **Set bonuses.**
  - The step bonuses of 36 item sets are now included.
  - Wearing enough pieces of a set adds that step's stats, spell power, healing, hit, crit and armor.
  - The optimizer also tries completing each set, which a slot-by-slot comparison would miss, and still breaks a set when other gear is better.
  - 70 bonuses that cannot be calculated, such as procs and resistances, are left out.
- **What you see.**
  - The summary shows the spell power used, or healing power for healers.
  - Recommended sets are shown as "Set name 2/3", with the number of left-out proc effects.
  - Item rows show "set n/m".
- **Decimal effects.** Gear effects with decimals, such as "hit 0.3%" or "crit 1.0%", are now read and counted.

## Windows app

- **Windows app 0.12.4** now installs addon 0.9.7.
