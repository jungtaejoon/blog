## New

- **Recipe item sources built into the addon.**
  - Drops with their drop chances, vendors with place and price, quest rewards and containers for 1,320 recipe items.
  - Shown in recipe item tooltips in your bags, in "How to learn" of unlearned recipes and in the learning details window.
  - The craft-uses list (Alt on a material) shows the kinds in one line, such as "Recipe item · Drop/Sells · skill 100".
- **The game's own names.**
  - On a Korean client, zone, item, currency and container names come from the Forever client data.
  - Most creature and vendor names are shown in Korean too, and quest titles are loaded from the game.
  - An English client shows the English names.
- **New Forever vendors are named in game.** Vendors added in Forever, such as the ones selling recipes for Merchant's Favor, have no Korean name in any data yet. Open their shop once and ForgeFlow remembers the name the game shows.

## Good to know

- The source data comes from the Forever fan site [ForeverChanges](https://foreverchanges.pro), which records what players saw in game (as of 2026-11-20). Tooltips name the source.
- When the game tooltip shows its own "Forever sources" block, that comes first.
- Lines marked "Classic data" come from Classic and may differ in Forever.

## Windows app

- **Windows app 0.12.5** now installs addon 0.9.11. If you use 0.12.5, the app offers the new build.
