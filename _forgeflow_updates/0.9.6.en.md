## Changed

- **A smaller main window.** It opens at 86% of its old size, so its text matches the game's own windows, and it never takes more than 92% of the screen width or 84% of its height. The window size setting (90–130%) scales from there.
- **A new window style.**
  - A smooth dark frame with a thin gold edge replaces the stone background.
  - The large icon that hung off the corner is now a small icon in the title bar.
  - The sidebar is slimmer, and the open page has a gold bar.
  - Cards and buttons have softer edges.
- **Twelve menu entries become eight.** Pages used together share one entry, and you switch between them with tabs in the title bar.
  - **Crafting workshop:** cost · prices, routes, interest list
  - **Search · buy** (auction house): item search, listings · buy, supply crates
  - Gear, prices · shopping, ledger, crafted goods, sell and my auctions are unchanged.
  - Purchases, sales and the recovery of uncertain trades work exactly as before.
- **Skill route panel.** The route panel beside the profession window has the same style. It is wider so lines no longer overlap, and each stretch has a bar in its skill colour (orange, yellow or green). The sale mode and price lookup are buttons at the bottom.
- **Merchant shortfall window.**
  - Change the craft count with −/+ next to the recipe name.
  - Each material shows its icon, need, owned and short amount, price and a buy button on one line.
  - The total and "Buy all shortfalls" are at the bottom.
  - It still buys only on your click, only in whole merchant bundles and never in combat.

## Windows app

- **Windows app 0.12.4** now installs addon 0.9.6.
