## New

- **Waylaid Supplies crates.** A new "Supply crates" page in the auction house sidebar. For the crates in your bags it searches only the materials and quantities they accept, compares the total cost of each option, and marks the cheapest. Press buy and the purchase screen opens with the missing quantity filled in; the purchase itself happens only when you press that screen's final button.
- **Recipes with no item output.** 183 recipes such as enchants are now listed in the profession window with their materials.

## Changed

- **The profession filter was rebuilt.**
  - Pick the learning state (all, learned, unlearned, unknown) right at the top of the menu. The menu stays open while you choose.
  - "Skill-ups" follows the list's skill-up arrow. Grey recipes are left out, learned or not.
  - New "Learnable at current skill" condition.
  - Skill-ups, learnable at current skill and craftable from bags all apply together (intersection); favorites and watched recipes show a recipe in either list (union).
  - Entries that repeated the game's own filters are gone.
- **Recipe data** updated to Forever client build 1.60.1.70862. The catalog is now compared with the newest build every day, and an update is prepared when something changed.
