## Fixed

- **Supply crate amounts.** The auction house "Supply crates" tab showed 1 as the amount needed for every material.
  - The crate tooltip lists all its options in one text, and a bundle progress in it (0/1) was read as the amount for every option.
  - Each option now reads only its own line. The Apprentice Herbs crate, for example, takes 20 Peacebloom, 20 Silverleaf, 10 Earthroot, 10 Mageroyal or 10 Briarthorn.
  - Bundles already inside the crate count as "inside", and only the shortfall is offered to buy.

## Windows app

- **Windows app 0.12.5** now installs addon 0.9.12. If you use 0.12.5, the app offers the new build.
