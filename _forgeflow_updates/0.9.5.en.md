## New

- **Crafted items and dungeon drops together.** Gear by level now covers 1,320 crafted items and 1,178 drops from 28 WoW Forever dungeons and raids. Stats are the real Forever values, shown with the boss and the drop chance. Raids are optional.
- **Damage and healing simulation.** For each slot it picks the set with the highest expected DPS (damage dealers and tanks) or HPS (healers).
  - It counts weapons, attack power, hit, dodge, crit and its multiplier, and spell crit and hit.
  - It tries both a two-hander and one-hand + off-hand; shield tanks keep a shield.
  - At your own level it starts from what you wear, swaps only what is better and shows the gain per slot.
- **Your learned talents.** Talents count at the rank you learned them. The values are read from the in-game talent descriptions, and the summary names the talents that counted.
- **Forever spell data.**
  - Casters and healers try every rank of their main spells, each with its real Forever amount, spell power coefficient, mana cost and cast time, and keep the rank that does the most within their mana.
  - Hunters fire Arcane Shot and Multi-Shot or Aimed Shot within their mana. Arcane Shot has no spell power coefficient in the Forever data, so spell power is not counted for it.
  - Paladins use Seal of Righteousness and Judgement; shamans use Earth Shock.
  - The summary shows the spells assumed and how long the mana lasts.
- **Alternative item cards per slot.** "Alt N ▼" opens cards for every item within 3, 5 or 10% of the best. Each card shows the icon, source, drop chance and the loss.
- **Alternative recipes in the skill route.** Each stretch lists other recipes that give a point over the whole stretch. The cards show expected crafts, net cost and the extra over the cheapest, within 10, 25 or 50%.

## Note

- Set bonuses, procs, buffs and enchants are not counted yet. Items with a proc are marked "Special effect not counted".
